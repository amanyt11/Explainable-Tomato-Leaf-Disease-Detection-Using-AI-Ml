Pathology Taxonomy
10 Monitored Tomato Foliar Conditions
Bacterial, Fungal & Oomycete Pathogens
  • Bacterial Spot (Xanthomonas vesicatoria): Dark spots with yellow chlorotic halos.
  • Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Concentric brown rings ('target board') on lower canopy.
  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Water-soaked dark grease lesions, rapid rot.
  • Leaf Mold (Passalora fulva): Pale upper spots, olive-velvety spores beneath.
  • Septoria Leaf Spot (Septoria lycopersici): Sunken circular spots with gray centers.
Pests, Viruses & Healthy Foliage
  • Spider Mites (Tetranychus urticae): Pinpoint yellow stippling and delicate webbing.
  • Target Spot (Corynespora cassiicola): Zoned circular lesions with cracked centers.
  • Yellow Leaf Curl (TYLCV): Upward leaf rolling ('spooning'), severe interveinal chlorosis.
  • Mosaic Virus (ToMV): Alternating light/dark green mosaic patches, leaf distortion.
  • Healthy Leaf: Vibrant green lamina, uniform venation, serrated intact margins.
Model Engineering
Convolutional Transfer Learning with EfficientNet-B0
EfficientNet-B0 Backbone
  • Compound Scaling: Jointly scales depth, width, and resolution with fixed scaling coefficient.
  • MBConv Blocks: Mobile inverted bottleneck convolutions with depthwise separable layers.
  • Squeeze-and-Excitation: Self-attention dynamically re-weights informative spatial channels.
  • Transfer Learning: Pretrained ImageNet feature representations accelerate convergence on small datasets.
Training Dynamics
  • Optimization: AdamW optimizer with decoupled weight decay (1e-4) to suppress overfitting.
  • Loss Function: Multi-class Cross-Entropy across 10 mutually exclusive categories.
  • Stratification: Seeded 80/20 train/validation split ensuring identical class frequencies.
  • Hardware Support: Auto-detects Apple Silicon MPS, NVIDIA CUDA, or CPU fallback.
Explainable AI Methodology
Grad-CAM: Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping
The Mathematics of Visual Attention Extraction
  • Step 1 · Feature Capture: Forward pass extracts feature activation maps Ak from the final convolutional layer.
  • Step 2 · Class Backpropagation: Gradients of predicted class score Yc are computed back to Ak: ∂Yc / ∂Ak.
  • Step 3 · Global Pooling: Channel importance weights αkc = (1/Z) ∑i ∑j (∂Yc / ∂Ai,jk).
  • Step 4 · Rectified Linear Combination: LGrad-CAMc = ReLU(∑k αkc Ak), keeping only positive contributions.
  • Step 5 · Spatial Upscaling & Colormap: Bilinear interpolation scales heatmap back to 224×224, mapped via Jet or Turbo colormaps for clear inspection.

Performance Validation
10-Class Confusion Matrix & Pathology Error Dynamics
Error Dynamics & Agronomic Findings
  • Early vs Late Blight (11%–12% overlap): Highest mutual error due to shared dark brown necrotic lesion presentation.
  • Bacterial Spot vs Septoria (7%–8% overlap): Secondary error in young seedling phases due to pinpoint spots (<3mm).
  • Highest Precision (82%–85%): Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV) and Healthy foliage exhibit distinct morphology.
  • Clinical Utility: Grad-CAM allows growers to immediately differentiate concentric rings from greasy water-soaked edges.
Comparative Analysis
Multi-Model Architecture Benchmarks & Edge Tradeoffs
Comparative Convolutional Backbones
  • EfficientNet-B0 (Baseline): 5.3M parameters | 20.4 MB | 14.2 ms latency | 65.9% val accuracy.
    → Best for Cloud APIs, high Grad-CAM spatial resolution, and low inference memory.
  • ResNet-18: 11.7M parameters | 44.8 MB | 11.5 ms latency | 63.4% val accuracy.
    → Fast convergence and straightforward residual skip connections.
  • ResNet-50: 25.6M parameters | 97.8 MB | 28.6 ms latency | 67.1% val accuracy.
    → Highest capacity for complex multi-symptom leaves and subtle co-infections.
  • MobileNetV3-Large: 5.4M parameters | 21.6 MB | 8.9 ms latency | 64.8% val accuracy.
    → Optimized for battery-powered edge farm devices, drones, and handheld scanners.
Actionable Prescriptions
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Agronomic Rx
🚨 Immediate Field Triage
  • Rogue and burn viral plants (TYLCV / ToMV) to halt reservoir spread.
  • Halt overhead sprinkler watering to stop spore splashing.
  • Sanitize tools with 70% alcohol or 20% skim milk solution.
🌿 Certified Organic Rx
  • OMRI-listed fixed copper sprays (hydroxide/octanoate) for Bacterial Spot.
  • Bio-fungicides containing Bacillus subtilis and Trichoderma.
  • Neem oil extracts & predatory mite releases against Spider Mites.
🧪 Chemical & Cultural
  • Rotate FRAC groups (chlorothalonil M05 with azoxystrobin 11).
  • Apply barrier mulch to block rain splashing from infected soil.
  • Enforce 3-year non-Solanaceous crop rotations.
